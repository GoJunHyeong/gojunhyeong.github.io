Research

Graduate researcher at the Computational Intelligence Lab, Ajou University, since 2021, advised by Prof. Jongbin Ryu.

Vision-language-action

  • FlowVLA — training-only 3D flow supervision that distills scene-level motion into TCP-aligned control dynamics, improving manipulation and zero-shot robustness with no 3D sensors or inference overhead. [C.2]
  • Structure-constrained trajectory perturbation that expands supervision around expert demonstrations while preserving cumulative motion and grasp/release events, with no additional data collection, parameters, or inference overhead. In preparation.
  • An asymmetric VLA action decoder that dynamically selects control-relevant modalities and allocates expert capacity by action granularity for high-fidelity manipulation. Co-developing, in preparation.
  • A two-loop VLA inference framework that cuts redundant computation by updating action-relevant states without re-running the full policy. Co-developing, in preparation.
  • A VLA training framework robust to missing modalities in real-world deployment. Co-developing, in preparation.

Vision applications

  • Channel Propagation — channel propagation networks that make Vision Transformer representations refreshable across layers, improving accuracy without changing the backbone at inference. [C.1]
  • Spatial Bias — a lightweight, attention-free spatial bias that adds global context to convolutional features, capturing long-range dependencies with about 10× fewer parameters and 1.6–3.3× higher throughput than self-attention-based non-local networks. [J.1]
  • DLPB — teacher-free online knowledge distillation with lightweight single-layer peer branches and decorrelation learning that diversify representations, improving modern vision backbones with minimal training overhead and no inference cost. [S.1]

Time-series forecasting

  • An LLM-based forecasting framework that contextualizes trend and seasonal structure instead of using LLMs for direct numerical prediction. Co-developing, in preparation.
